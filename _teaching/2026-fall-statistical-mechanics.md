Teaching Assistant for **PHYS 6611-001 (202630): Statistical Mechanics**, Fall 2026, taught by Prof. Randall (Randy) Kamien.

An introductory graduate course in statistical mechanics, using Pathria's *Statistical Mechanics* as the text. Topics include:

- Hamiltonian dynamics, Liouville's theorem, and the ergodic theorem
- Microcanonical, canonical, and grand canonical ensembles; thermodynamics
- Cluster and virial expansions; quantum statistics of fermions and bosons
- The Ising model, high- and low-temperature expansions, phase transitions, critical phenomena, and universality
- Random walks, path integrals, the diffusion equation, and the Fokker–Planck and Smoluchowski equations
- An application to finance: the Black–Scholes option pricing model
